🌱 EcoQuest
Learn. Play. Make a Greener Tomorrow.

EcoQuest is a gamified environmental education platform designed to make learning about sustainability more interactive, engaging, and practical.

Instead of traditional environment-related learning, EcoQuest combines courses, quizzes, challenges, rewards, streaks, badges, and leaderboards to encourage students to learn environmental concepts and develop sustainable habits.

---

🎯 Problem

Environmental education is often presented through textbooks, lectures, and awareness campaigns, which can make it difficult for students to stay engaged or connect what they learn with real-world actions.

EcoQuest aims to make environmental learning more interactive by turning it into a game-like experience where users can:

- Learn environmental concepts
- Test their knowledge through quizzes
- Complete sustainability challenges
- Earn XP and rewards
- Maintain learning streaks
- Unlock badges and achievements
- Compete on leaderboards
- Track their environmental impact

---

💡 Our Solution

EcoQuest provides a single platform where environmental education and gamification come together.

Users progress through different environmental topics, complete activities, earn experience points, unlock achievements, and participate in challenges.

Main Learning Areas

- 🌍 Climate Change
- 🌳 Biodiversity & Ecosystems
- ♻️ Waste Management
- 💧 Water Conservation
- ⚡ Renewable Energy
- 🌱 Sustainable Living
- 🐾 Environmental Protection

---

✨ Key Features

📚 Interactive Learning

Topic-based learning modules with short, easy-to-understand content.

🧠 Quizzes

Interactive quizzes to test understanding and earn XP.

🔥 Streaks

Daily learning streaks encourage users to return regularly.

🏆 XP & Levels

Users earn XP by completing lessons, quizzes, and challenges and progress through different levels.

🎖️ Badges & Achievements

Users can unlock badges for completing milestones and special activities.

🌎 Eco Challenges

Users can participate in sustainability-related challenges such as recycling, saving water, reducing waste, and planting trees.

🥇 Leaderboard

Users can compare their XP and achievements with classmates and other participants.

👤 Profile & Progress

A personal dashboard showing:

- Current level
- XP
- Streak
- Completed courses
- Completed challenges
- Earned badges
- Environmental impact

👥 Community

A space where users can share achievements, participate in discussions, and interact with other learners.

📊 Impact Tracking

EcoQuest represents sustainable actions through measurable impact indicators such as virtual trees planted, waste reduced, water saved, or equivalent environmental points.

For the backend, follow the setup instructions inside the "/backend" directory.
